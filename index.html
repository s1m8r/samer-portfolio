+A little about me

I'm Samer Al-Musawi, a front-end developer based in Baghdad. I care about the layer between a design file and something a person can actually click through — the spacing, the state changes, the parts that are easy to skip.

Most of what I build runs on React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, with TanStack handling data and routing. I'd rather ship something small that's easy to change later than something large that isn't.
